# Homeward Health

> Homeward Health is a value-based, full-risk healthcare provider (founded 2022, CEO Dr. Jennifer Schneider) rearchitecting rural healthcare for Medicare Advantage members via telehealth over cellular networks, in-home remote patient monitoring, and mobile care units, in partnership with regional health plans. Homeward is a care-delivery company and publishes no public developer API, SDK, or MCP server.
